Stickman Hit / 1v1 fighter roster / draft 2

Pick your stickman

Four fighters, each drawn as a 24×24 pixel sprite with 7 poses: idle, two walk frames, jump, attack, getting hit and victory. Names are placeholders until the final names are picked.

How the roster is balanced

  • Every fighter gets exactly 12 stat points spread across Power, Speed, Reach and Defense, so nobody is simply better than the others.
  • Each fighter has one clear strength. BOXER is the even starter, NINJA is the fastest, SAMURAI has strong hits with good reach, and ROBOT has the most armor.
  • Rough matchups: BOXER beats SAMURAI (gets inside the sword), SAMURAI beats ROBOT (Counter Slash punishes the slow Rocket Punch), ROBOT beats NINJA (armor shrugs off quick hits), NINJA beats BOXER (too fast to hit).
