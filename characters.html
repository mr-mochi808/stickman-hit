Stickman Hit / 1v1 fighter roster / draft 1

Pick your stickman

Four fighters with the same plain stick body. What tells them apart is their gear: a headband, a hard hat, a ninja mask and a cap. Every sprite is 16×16 pixels, shown here at 6× zoom and at real size.

How the roster is balanced

  • Every fighter gets exactly 12 stat points spread across Power, Speed, Reach and Defense, so nobody is simply better than the others.
  • Each fighter has one clear strength and one clear weakness. BONK is very strong but very slow. ZIP is very fast but easy to knock out. TWIG has very long reach but is weak up close. JAB is the middle-of-the-road starter.
  • Rough matchups: JAB beats TWIG (gets in close), TWIG beats BONK (keeps him at a distance), BONK beats ZIP (one hit is enough), ZIP beats JAB (too fast to hit).
